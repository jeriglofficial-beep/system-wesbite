System rules
Read these when motivation disappears — especially then
1
Show up every day, even for 20 minutes.
Consistency beats intensity. The system survives missed days, not missed weeks. Never miss twice in a row.
2
The 3 hours are sacred — protect them.
Morning skill block + afternoon work block. Guard these from social media, chores, and distractions. Everything else happens around them.
3
Gym is not optional. It is infrastructure.
2 hours of training daily isn't extra — it is the foundation that makes everything else possible. Never skip it for "work".
4
BCA comes first during college hours.
Attend every class. Submit every project. Use free periods wisely. The degree is a credential — don't let your business dreams hurt it.
5
Pick one agency niche and go deep.
SEO, paid ads, social media, or web design — pick one by end of July. Generalists earn less. Specialists get paid.
6
Done beats perfect. Ship it.
Post the design. Push the code. Send the DM. Imperfect work that exists beats perfect work that doesn't.
7
Music on commute is protected time.
7:30–9:00 AM is your decompression. No podcasts about productivity. No guilt. Just music and presence.
8
Family and friends are not distractions.
4–6 PM belongs to people. Be present. Relationships you build now will matter more at 30 than any skill you learned at 19.
9
Review every Sunday. Adjust. Never abandon.
The system is designed to evolve. When something isn't working, fix that thing — don't throw out the whole system.
10
Your body is the machine. Sleep is maintenance.
10:30 PM lights out. 7–8 hours of sleep is not a luxury — it is the prerequisite for everything on this page.
